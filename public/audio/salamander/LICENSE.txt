Chordflow redistributes 30 existing MP3 files, unchanged, as a single-layer
piano instrument. See manifest.json for each original URL and SHA-256.
Playback repitches the nearest sample by at most one semitone, scales gain
for velocity and applies a short key-release envelope. It does not implement
the original SFZ's 16 velocity layers, pedal noises or resonance layers.
